CHAPTER 01 // THE TOKYO GENESIS1980s TOKYO

Relentless Determination in Japan

The story of Elock is not just a business narrative; it’s a testament to perseverance, resilience, and the unyielding pursuit of excellence. The company’s journey began in 1990, but its roots trace back to its founder, Michael Teo, and his relentless determination in Japan years earlier. Back then, Michael was working four part-time jobs daily, navigating the bustling streets of Tokyo. Each day, he would juggle strenuous work at dawn, noon, and late into the night, just to save every yen he could.

CHAPTER 02 // RETURNING HOME & ESTABLISHING ELOCKPUCHONG, 1990

Kyodensha Technologies (M) Sdn Bhd

Returning to Malaysia with hard-won capital and industrial insight, Michael founded Kyodensha Technologies in 1990. Rather than simply reselling cheap imported commodities, he spearheaded homegrown electromagnetic locking innovation: winding proprietary triple-insulated copper coils, integrating silicon steel armatures, and mastering instant zero-residual demagnetization release hardware.
